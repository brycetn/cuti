Clinical Decision Support

cUTI Likelihood Decision Tool for NLUTD due to SCI/D

A structured symptom-based tool to estimate the likelihood of complicated urinary tract infection in patients with neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction.

Intended Population

People with neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction (NLUTD) due to spinal cord injury or disease (SCI/D), regardless of bladder-management method.

Clinical Disclaimer

This tool supports, but does not replace, clinician judgment. Clinicians should evaluate urgent or severe symptoms according to local clinical protocols. It should not be used as the sole basis for diagnosis or treatment.

Reference

This clinical tool is based upon: Tractenberg RE, Aguirre Guemez AV, Petriello M, Groah SL. (2025). SCI Model Systems Complicated UTI (cUTI) Guidelines: International Consensus on cUTI Likelihood Based on Symptoms. Topics in Spinal Cord Injury Rehabilitation 2025 Summer; 31(3):101–120. doi: 10.46292/sci25-00019.

Development

Developed by Thomas N. Bryce, Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai. This tool was created with AI-assisted development using Microsoft Copilot. The tool uses predefined, rule-based decision logic; AI does not make patient-specific decisions during use.
